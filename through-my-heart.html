Through my heart

प्रतिक अक्कावार 13 March 2013 less than a minute

When I sit all alone
thinking of life I’m living
all I could remember are

the people who surround me
the relations I have with them
the love I receive from each one
the feelings I have for them
the moments I cherish
the memories they create

then I do try to put these things together
hoping that nothing will ever depart
but I could only manage to write some words
that comes straight through my heart
