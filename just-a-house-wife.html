Just a house wife

प्रतिक अक्कावार December 28, 2014

She wakes up early in the morning before anyone else wakes up
She keeps a cup of hot tea ready for you when you come out taking bath
She keeps your breakfast ready before you leave to school, college or office
She hands over every little thing you need before you go out
She serves you lunch and dinner on time every day
She washes clothes and utensils, she keeps the house clean
She goes to shop, she pays the house bills
She prays for you, she prays for a family
She cares for you, she cares for a family
She loves you, she loves a family
She suffers a lot and feels the pain
She makes sacrifices but do not complain
She bears your yell but do not shout
She is literate but do not go out
Who is she? Just a house wife…!!!
