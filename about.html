About Nishabd

Welcome to my personal blogging website ‘Nishabd – words from my heart’. Nishabd is all about my life and my journey as a writer/poet. I publish here my poems, stories and thoughts, in short the words from my heart. I believe that if you put your thoughts, feelings or experiences into words they can make beautiful poems or sweet stories that are worth to share with the world. Words have power to inspire and motivate the people.

I publish the content mainly in three languages viz. Marathi, Hindi and English because I believe writing is an art that knows no language barriers. The writing content is organized into the categories that best fit in. If you are not able to decide where to start from, below are links to some specific pages.
